# Market Research

## Purpose
Describe the real market the business can serve, not the largest market that can be imagined. Identify demand signals, constraints and actionable opportunities for the Director.

## Evidence-led investigation
- Define the offering, customer use case, geography/service radius, sales channel, relevant period and decision. Use approved Brain and existing evidence first; raise material missing context through Director. Adapt depth to the decision, not a preset research mode.
- Discover category terminology and customer search language in Arabic, Egyptian colloquial, English and relevant transliterations. Search beyond the first plausible query when material gaps remain; avoid repeating equivalent searches. Read relevant primary pages and track coverage gaps.
- Look for actual transactions or documented demand where accessible, official economic/sector publications, business reports, product availability, customer questions and seasonality. Distinguish demand indicators from measured purchases; search interest, followers and listing counts are proxies, not market revenue.
- For size, distinguish total addressable market, realistically serviceable market and obtainable share when those concepts serve the decision. Prefer defined, dated estimates. If estimating from the bottom up, state population/unit counts, eligible share, buying frequency, average spend, geography, currency, formula and assumptions. Cross-check plausibility and avoid overlapping segments. Use ranges/sensitivity only when inputs support them; otherwise report size as unknown. Do not invent precision or silently extrapolate foreign statistics.

## Egyptian context
Use relevant Egyptian sources (e.g. CAPMAS, CBE, sector authorities) through ordinary search and reading, not a fixed source registry. Separate national indicators from neighborhood or governorate demand. Consider local delivery and collection costs, distribution, informal competitors, cash/digital payment options, household affordability, inflation and seasonal changes as hypotheses to investigate, not universal truths. Ramadan, Eid, school seasons and tourism matter only for relevant categories and dates. A missing website is not proof that a business or market is absent.

## Return and self-review
Return scope, supported demand and trend findings, size or limitations, segments, barriers, opportunities, decision implications and evidence references. Identify material unknowns and the next economical validation step. Verify that time, geography, definitions and units match; stop when evidence meets the assignment or additional work is unlikely to change the decision within the budget. Do not execute the market entry strategy; Director owns that decision.
