# Competitor Intelligence

## Purpose
Explain who actually competes for the same customer's purchase, on what basis, and what the business can learn from the evidence.

## Discovery and qualification
- Start from the approved offering, use case, price segment, geography and delivery/service area. Search in the languages and terms buyers use; inspect relevant business websites, public social pages, storefronts, marketplaces and directories where allowed. Direct competitors address the same need for a materially overlapping customer; substitutes meet it differently. Record why each belongs and exclude irrelevant or ambiguous matches rather than inflating the list.
- Confirm identity with reliable signals such as official links, location, product/service and current activity. Resolve aliases, branches, duplicate listings and resellers without merging distinct businesses. A directory, review site or platform is generally a source, not a competitor, unless its own offering qualifies. A social-only seller can be a valid competitor. Mark unconfirmed identity explicitly.
- Compare only decision-relevant dimensions: products/services, target segment, offer, channel, service area, publicly observable messaging, fulfilment and proof. Distinguish seller claims from independent customer observations; do not infer private sales, ad spend, profit, traffic, share or targeting from public signals.

## Price discipline
Record exact observed amount, currency, package/quantity/variant, billing or service period, offer date and source; note whether taxes, delivery, installation, deposits or discounts are included or unknown. Egyptian prices may change rapidly. Distinguish list prices, promotional prices, starting prices, quotes and historical prices. If price is available only by inquiry, mark it as not publicly verified; do not contact businesses or invent it. Compare like units and terms; show transparent arithmetic and a dated verified FX basis if conversion is necessary. Do not assume a price from another country applies to Egypt. Avoid treating unknown costs as zero.

## Return and self-review
Return qualified competitors and rationale, relevant comparable observations, sourced price table where evidence exists, strengths/weaknesses with support, observed market gaps and uncertainties. Use comparable fields without forcing empty dimensions. Distinguish observable facts from hypotheses. Check identity, dates, geographic overlap and actual citation support. Report insufficient coverage honestly; do not claim exhaustive discovery or repeat queries just to reach a count. Director decides competitive strategy.
