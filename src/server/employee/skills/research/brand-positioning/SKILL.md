# Brand Positioning

## Purpose
Provide the Director with a defensible view of current market positioning and potential directions, rather than choosing or publishing the final brand strategy.

## Investigate positioning
- Start with the approved offering, demonstrated capabilities, existing brand claims, target customer and real alternatives. Retrieve competitor and audience evidence from the task or existing Deliverables before collecting more. Separate intended positioning (what the business says), observed public signals (what customers or materials indicate), and recommended positioning hypotheses.
- Understand the alternatives a buyer actually considers, including doing nothing, local substitutes and non-branded providers where relevant. Investigate which distinctive attributes produce meaningful customer benefits. Require proof: an observable capability, reliable customer evidence or approved business facts. Avoid treating generic claims such as best quality or lowest price as differentiation without evidence.
- Consider which segment values the difference, why it matters, and which category/frame makes the offer understandable. Explore plausible options rather than filling a rigid statement template. Where a positioning map helps, select observable meaningful axes (e.g. service speed versus range, or price tier versus service scope); disclose basis, avoid invented numeric placements and distinguish unknowns. Do not imply statistically measured perception from a few posts or reviews.
- Assess opportunity, credibility, competition and trade-offs. A gap in competitor messaging is a hypothesis, not proof of unmet demand. Check operational ability to deliver the promised experience, and do not claim unverified superiority or guarantees.

## Egyptian context
Consider local buying alternatives, trust signals, service accessibility, payment, affordability, product authenticity, delivery and after-sales support as possible differentiators, not assumed preferences. Distinguish Cairo-centric evidence from broader markets. Preserve authentic brand voice and audience terminology, without assuming that Egyptian identity requires slang or price-led positioning.

## Return and self-review
Return current positioning evidence, relevant alternatives, candidate differentiation, segment fit, proof available/needed, risks and ways to validate. Clearly label hypotheses and avoid finalizing brand decisions. The Director owns strategic positioning; Content Agent produces messaging and creative assets. No Brain changes without explicit owner approval.
