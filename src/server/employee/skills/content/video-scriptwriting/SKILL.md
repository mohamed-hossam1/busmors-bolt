# Video Scriptwriting

## Purpose
Create a script that conveys the assigned message in a feasible video format. The deliverable is production-ready text, not a generated or recorded video.

## Script design
- Identify the audience, objective, format, intended duration, platform, verified offer, tone, available footage/equipment and CTA. Resolve material missing information through Director. Select an appropriate creative structure such as demonstration, problem/solution, question/answer, credible story or concise explanation; do not use the same template for every topic.
- Open with a clear visual or spoken reason to watch, develop the message with relevant evidence or demonstration, and conclude with a natural next step where appropriate. Avoid clickbait hooks whose payoff is not delivered. Provide useful scenes in an order that supports comprehension and retention; approximate timing may be used but must be feasible for the words spoken.
- For each scene or beat, include approximate duration, visual action/shot, spoken words, on-screen text and optional sound or transition notes as useful. Distinguish exact spoken copy from production directions. Keep overlays legible, allow space for platform interface and include captions for essential spoken information. Design so the core message is understandable when muted where appropriate. Verify specific placement constraints rather than assuming timeless platform rules.
- Match creative demands to production capacity. A phone-shot demo, staff explanation or simple product close-up can be suitable. Flag missing assets, actor permissions, music/licensing requirements, locations, props or claims requiring review. Do not pretend an actor or customer provided a quote; no fake reviews, fake proof, unverified transformation claims or copyrighted audio instructions framed as licensed.

## Egyptian relevance
Write dialogue in the voice appropriate to the actual audience and brand; natural Egyptian speech is a choice, not a mandate. Read numbers, names and prices naturally and preserve exact offer conditions. Adapt examples to local situations when relevant without stereotypes or forced slang. Consider whether the intended response is an actual message, call, store visit or website action, and use only verified destinations.

## Return and self-review
Return the script, estimated duration, scene/shot notes, spoken copy, overlay text, CTA and production requirements. Include alternate hooks only when useful/requested and distinguish them from additional scripts. Check pacing, coherence, claim accuracy, voice and feasibility. Do not create media, publish, schedule or claim guaranteed retention or views. Director reviews the result.
