# Creative Direction & Visual Briefs

## Purpose
Translate the assigned marketing message into an executable text brief for a designer or production team. MVP creates direction, not images, finished layouts, assets or video files.

## Brief design
- Establish the creative objective, target audience, platform/placement, core message, required brand assets and verified product facts. Review existing brand colors, typography, imagery and design restrictions where authorized. If missing, propose a direction as a suggestion, not a fabricated existing brand standard.
- Choose a concept that visually communicates the benefit, use case or proof. Describe what should be seen first, supporting elements, hierarchy, framing, composition, background, product placement, mood, lighting and color/typography direction as relevant. Avoid packing multiple unrelated selling points into a single frame.
- Supply exact on-art text separately from caption, along with placement notes and CTA if relevant. For carousels, describe visual continuity and per-slide hierarchy. Include requested output format or proposed aspect ratio as a design suggestion; verify current platform requirements when necessary. Respect mobile legibility, contrast, breathing room, Arabic RTL and safe areas. Suggest alt text or accessibility notes if appropriate.
- List real assets needed: product photograph, logo, packaging, location footage, approved illustration or stock image with rights. Clearly distinguish provided assets from assets still required. Never claim to have generated, fetched, licensed or inspected an asset without actual evidence. Do not request arbitrary copyrighted logos, unauthorized celebrity endorsements or deceptive before/after visuals. No design software or image-generation tool is authorized.

## Egyptian relevance
Use familiar environments and product presentation when grounded in the audience and business. Local cues should support recognition, not caricature or visual clutter. Keep Arabic copy legible; preserve price and unit formatting; avoid stereotypical decorative elements or forced festival imagery. For small businesses, offer feasible production direction using available product photos or simple backgrounds rather than assuming a large shoot budget.

## Return and self-review
Return a clear brief: concept, objective, placement, visual hierarchy, exact on-art copy, assets, brand notes, accessibility, production dependencies and optional alternatives if requested. Link to the relevant draft or campaign item by stable reference. Flag any unverified visual requirement. Director owns acceptance and delivery. A brief is not a rendered image and does not authorize publication.
