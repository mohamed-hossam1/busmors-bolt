# Social Media Content

## Purpose
Create social content that advances the assigned objective and feels native to the intended audience and platform. A post should be useful or persuasive on its own, not just fill a calendar slot.

## Creative method
- Read the brief, approved Brain and relevant prior posts before writing. Identify the audience, awareness stage, content objective, offer, supporting facts, format and real destination for the call to action. Resolve material unknowns through Director; do not invent a promotion to make copy stronger.
- Choose an angle worth the audience's attention: a practical benefit, a real product demonstration, a common question, a verified story, an objection answered or a relevant seasonal need. Avoid mechanically using the same hook, caption formula or emotional trigger on every post.
- For a single post, supply the final caption, optional on-art text separately and an appropriate next action. For a carousel, make each slide advance the same idea; supply ordered slide text and a caption. For a story, define the frames and any interactive element as a suggestion, not as a deployed feature. If video is requested, load the video skill when useful.
- Adapt to actual placement: Facebook can accommodate service details and a local discussion; Instagram may need stronger visual context; LinkedIn may need a credible professional angle; short-form video needs an immediate reason to keep watching. These are creative options, not permanent platform rules. Verify current constraints if material. Use hashtags and emojis sparingly, only if consistent with brand and audience.

## Egyptian relevance
Choose Egyptian Arabic, accessible standard Arabic, English or a bilingual approach according to the audience and approved brand voice. Make prices, sizes, locations, delivery area and ordering instructions understandable in local terms only when supported. A Messenger, WhatsApp, in-store or website CTA must point to a real authorized destination; never fabricate a phone number or link. Do not force Ramadan references, slang or humor into unrelated content.

## Return and self-review
Return draft-ready text with platform, format, objective, CTA, relevant evidence and optional visual brief or dependencies. Separate caption from production notes; label variants as variants, not additional calendar posts. Confirm that facts, voice, format, audience and next action align. Flag unavailable assets or unsupported claims. Director owns review and persistence; never publish or schedule.
