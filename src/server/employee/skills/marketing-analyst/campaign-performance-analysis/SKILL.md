# Campaign Performance Analysis

## Method
- Identify campaign objective, account, campaign/ad identifiers, dates, currency, attribution settings and conversion event. Inspect data availability, grain, delivery status and reporting delays. Do not assume ad account access from a Page connection; use approved read actions or supplied reports only.
- Trace the measurable path from delivery to interaction to business outcome. Separate platform-attributed results from independently verified orders or CRM outcomes. Align spend and results to compatible periods and attribution basis; flag immature conversions and late reporting. Avoid joining datasets on approximate names or summing platform-attributed conversions across channels without proven deduplication.
- Where fields support it, calculate CPM, click rate, CPC, cost per specified result, conversion rates and attributed revenue per ad spend with calculator/data analysis. Label the actual click type, conversion action and value basis. Distinguish cost per lead from cost per delivered order and ROAS from profit. If spend or value is missing, do not compute a financial efficiency figure.
- Compare campaign performance with the assigned goal, constraints and verified baseline. Segment by campaign, audience, placement, creative or period only where sufficient data exists. Check that a change in mix, tracking, budget, offer or attribution did not create an apparent change in performance.

## Egyptian relevance
A cash-on-delivery order may be placed, confirmed, delivered, cancelled or returned. For service businesses an inquiry, qualified lead, appointment and attended appointment are different results. Preserve those distinctions and use EGP when supplied; no guessed exchange rates or assumed payment behavior. Reconcile offline outcomes only when records are authorized and matchable. Treat unmatched outcomes as unknown.

## Return and review
Provide campaign-level metrics with source, period, currency, denominator and attribution caveats; funnel losses; supported observations; alternative explanations; and practical recommendations. Identify missing ad or order data and request it through Director if essential. Never launch, pause, edit or fund ads, modify attribution settings or claim to prove incremental return from descriptive reporting.
