# Performance Optimization

## Method
- Start from the approved marketing objective, evidence and business capacity. Identify the bottleneck or opportunity most worth addressing. Recommend changes linked to observed findings, such as clearer offers, more informative product creative, improved response processes, tighter measurement or a better channel fit. Avoid generic growth tips or unsupported promises.
- Explain why each action may help, which outcome it targets, what assumptions it relies on, expected effort and meaningful tradeoffs. Prioritize qualitatively using evidence strength, likely impact, feasibility and reversibility. Do not fabricate numerical uplift forecasts or ICE scores. Quantify only when the data and assumptions support a labeled estimate.
- Propose an experiment when uncertainty materially affects a decision. Define a testable hypothesis, comparison or control, primary outcome, guardrail metrics, eligible population, measurement window and decision rule. If randomization or sufficient traffic is unavailable, propose a limited pilot or descriptive monitoring and disclose its weaker causal evidence. Use verified sample-size/statistical capabilities if needed; otherwise do not invent significance or test duration.
- Interpret experiments carefully. Check group assignment and data integrity, comparable exposure, conversion maturity, effect size and uncertainty. A mismatch in intended allocation warrants investigation. Avoid declaring winners based on early peeking, arbitrary thresholds or many unplanned segment tests. A non-significant result is not proof that two options are identical.

## Egyptian relevance
Match recommendations to the client's real resources: phone-based content production, branch capacity, response availability, delivery coverage, actual payment methods or sales process. Where orders are fulfilled offline, measure downstream outcomes and monitor cancellations or complaints when available. Choose a low-cost measurement improvement if current tracking cannot establish the effect.

## Return and review
Return prioritized action proposals, supporting evidence, dependencies, proposed success and guardrail metrics, and a practical validation plan. Clearly separate immediate low-risk recommendations from hypotheses requiring testing. Director owns final strategic decisions and may delegate creative production. Recommendations are not executed actions: no campaign edits, budget changes, publishing, tracking deployment or paid tests. Never promise a specific improvement without evidence.
