# Content Performance Analysis

## Method
- Start with the objective and evaluation grain. Link published post identifiers to Calendar records or verified exports. Drafts, scheduled but unpublished posts and missing rows are not observed performance. Distinguish post publication date from the date range of measured activity.
- Group comparable content by objective, platform, format, topic, offer, creative angle or CTA when fields exist. When tagging from text or creative metadata, label inferred categories; do not claim to have seen unavailable images or video. Retain stable item IDs and distinguish versions.
- Compare exposure and outcome together. Normalize by relevant denominator and align time since publication where possible, especially for recent posts. Account for paid promotion, distribution, audience size, placement and unequal sample counts. Use totals, per-item distributions and denominators as appropriate; avoid simple averages of unequal rates when a weighted aggregate is intended.
- For videos inspect comparable retention or completion definitions if present. For conversion content distinguish link clicks, inquiries and verified downstream actions. Avoid judging educational or awareness content solely by orders unless that was its goal.

## Egyptian relevance
Use the actual approved offer, brand voice and local audience. Explore whether content about delivery, trust, product demonstration, pricing or service locations is associated with the intended outcomes; do not assume these themes work universally. Compare relevant seasonal periods, e.g. Ramadan or Eid, only when dates and business circumstances support it. Do not attribute results to colloquial Arabic or English based on a handful of posts.

## Return and review
Return an item-level or segment-level summary with metric definitions, comparable cohorts, standout and weaker examples, plausible explanations and a small set of concrete creative recommendations for Director. Identify the exact posts and evidence behind each recommendation. Label differences as descriptive unless supported by a valid experiment. If the sample is small or missing creative attributes, limit conclusions and suggest what to measure next. Use calculation tools and reconcile counts. Do not write replacement campaigns or edit published posts; Director may request creative work from Content Agent.
