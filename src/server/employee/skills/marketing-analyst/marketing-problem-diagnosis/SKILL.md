# Marketing Problem Diagnosis

## Method
- Define the problem precisely: outcome, affected scope, start date, baseline, magnitude and business impact. Confirm that the measurement itself is trustworthy before explaining the change. Check missing days, changed definitions, conversion lag, duplicate rows, tracking changes and incomplete cohorts.
- Examine the relevant funnel and segment the change by time, campaign, channel, product, audience or geography only when available. Separate volume changes from rate changes. Use verified calculations to identify which stage contributes to the observed outcome. Compare against a reasonable prior period, controlling for exposure and eligibility where possible.
- Develop a small set of plausible explanations: distribution, creative mismatch, audience fit, unclear offer, response delay, landing-page friction, stock, delivery, price, tracking or external context. Treat them as alternatives, not a mandatory checklist or a proven causal chain. Trace each explanation to supporting and contradictory observations. Ask what would disconfirm it and what missing evidence would distinguish competing explanations.
- Distinguish a descriptive association, a hypothesis, an independently verified operational issue and a causal conclusion from an appropriate experiment. Do not blame staff, platform algorithms or competitors without evidence. Do not claim a root cause merely because a five-whys exercise reaches one.

## Egyptian relevance
For a local business, a fall in delivered orders may reflect fewer inquiries, slower responses, changes in confirmation, cancellations, unavailable stock or courier/service-area constraints. Consider actual local dates, holidays, price changes and market conditions only where supported. Avoid treating country-wide inflation or seasonality as a default explanation for a specific decline.

## Return and review
State the symptom and measured change, the most relevant affected stages, supported facts, competing hypotheses, confidence limitations, and targeted validation requests. Prioritize next checks by likely decision value and feasibility. If a cause remains unverified, say so and suggest a low-risk measurement or experiment. Route external market evidence requests and substantive strategic decisions through Director. No automatic changes to accounts, campaigns, staffing or operations.
