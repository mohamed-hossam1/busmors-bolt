# Egyptian Market & Business Intelligence

## Purpose
Adapt marketing judgment to the actual Egyptian business and its serviceable market, without stereotyping or claiming that broad market statistics describe a particular customer.

## Local context lenses
- Define the business model (retail, local service, ecommerce, B2B or other), actual selling and delivery area, buyer situation and available resources. Distinguish neighborhood, city, governorate and national claims. Check whether the business serves Greater Cairo, other cities or rural locations rather than presuming one national market.
- Assess purchasing constraints: relevant EGP price point, affordability, payment choices, delivery charges, return conditions and confirmed customer objections. Cash on delivery, wallets, cards and installments are possibilities, not default assumptions. Check what the merchant actually supports.
- Map how customers discover, evaluate and buy: local referrals, store visits, search/maps, social platforms, messaging, marketplaces or direct sales as relevant. Facebook and WhatsApp may be useful, but verify audience fit; recommending a channel is not evidence of a Busmora integration.
- Consider timing (e.g. Ramadan, Eid, school periods, summer, pay cycles) only when relevant. Verify current dates, sector behavior and operational readiness. Do not infer that every sector peaks at the same time.
- Consider Arabic register, trust signals, location/accessibility, customer support, response speed, delivery coverage and offline-to-online handoffs. Avoid unsupported generalizations based on income, location or social class.

## Evidence and updating
Prefer the business's approved records and first-party sales or customer observations for business-specific facts. For national or regional context, search for relevant CAPMAS, CBE, official or credible sector publications using approved web tools. Check publication date, measurement period, definition, sample and geography. A bank-account statistic is not a measure of ecommerce use. Verify current prices, exchange rates, legal constraints or platform availability before making consequential claims. Never embed a fixed statistics table in the skill.

## Output
Explain the local condition that changes the decision, the evidence or assumption behind it, and the practical adaptation. Recommend a focused test when local evidence is thin. Send extensive market mapping or segmentation evidence collection to Research Agent; do not treat this skill as a substitute for market research.

## Quality check
Is each local claim bounded to its evidence? Is the recommendation executable with the business's actual channels, staff and service area? Is the language natural rather than caricatured?
