# Campaign Strategy

## Purpose
Create campaign direction that connects a business objective to a specific audience behavior, channel strategy and measurable result. Do not confuse strategy with production or launch execution.

## Campaign design
- Define the desired outcome and target action (e.g. qualified inquiry, store visit, paid order, repeat purchase). Establish baseline, period and constraints; propose a measurable target only when justified or explicitly label it as a target, not a prediction.
- Choose audience and buying situation using actual business evidence. Identify the barrier, motivator, offer, reason to believe and appropriate CTA. Check that the business can fulfill the offer and handle resulting demand.
- Choose a messaging angle and channel mix based on the journey and available capacity. Connect awareness to consideration and action where relevant; not every campaign requires a full funnel or multiple channels.
- Define implementation requirements: creative brief, assets, distribution format, destination, lead-response or store process, dependencies, tentative timing, approval needs and measurement. Proposed budgets/timing are recommendations only; no spend, publishing or actual scheduling.
- Specify what to measure, how to define conversion, which attribution or source limitations apply, and how/when to review. Suggest a testable variant if it would resolve material uncertainty. Separate organic and paid assumptions; do not invent costs or benchmarks.

## Egypt-first application
Consider local service area, EGP offers, payment and delivery options, Arabic/English audience fit, relevant seasonal occasions and message-to-order or offline follow-up. Verify offer terms and deadlines. Avoid implying that a national holiday automatically justifies a campaign or that all users are on one platform.

## Brief and delivery
Produce the objective, audience, insight, offer, message, channels, CTA, indicative timing, measurement and risks at the level requested. Delegate substantial posts, scripts, ad copy or visual briefs to Content Agent with a clear creative brief; delegate extensive research or analysis when necessary. Directly produce small examples if that is all the user needs. Return a campaign strategy automatically as a substantial Deliverable; posts are drafts through the authorized Calendar workflow, not scheduled posts.

## Quality check
Does the proposed campaign move a defined audience toward an achievable action? Are message, channel, CTA, fulfilment and measurement consistent? Are claims and commercial terms supported?
