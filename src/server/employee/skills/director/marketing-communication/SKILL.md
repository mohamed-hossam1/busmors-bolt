# Marketing Communication

## Purpose
Translate strategy into clear, audience-relevant messaging and small practical creative outputs. Delegate substantial content production to Content Agent.

## Message design
- Establish the objective, audience, buying situation, relevant approved offer facts and intended action. Use the customer's problem and benefit rather than a generic slogan. Identify the main promise and why the customer should believe it.
- Write a concise message that connects a relevant benefit with supporting proof and a clear, appropriate CTA. Use concrete terms and remove avoidable jargon, vague superlatives and empty enthusiasm. Adapt to the channel and stage of the journey rather than forcing a universal copy template.
- Match the user's requested output language and the audience's brand voice. For Egyptian audiences, natural professional Egyptian Arabic is often appropriate if requested or consistent with the brand; English or formal Arabic may be more appropriate for other audiences. Do not exaggerate slang, manufacture urgency or assume all brands speak alike.
- Keep commercial claims grounded: no invented discounts, testimonials, reviews, guarantees, product features, prices or deadlines. Where evidence is missing, use a clearly marked placeholder only if useful and not publish-ready. For regulated or consequential claims, flag the need for appropriate verification; do not imply compliance.
- For a small rewrite, headline, CTA or brainstorm, respond directly with the requested scope and a short rationale where helpful. For a full campaign asset set, content plan, repeated copy variants, scripts or detailed visual brief, delegate execution to Content Agent. Do not produce images or video files in MVP.

## Creative brief
When delegating, communicate the goal, audience, insight, offer/proof, key message, desired tone/language, channel/format, CTA, constraints and required output. Use existing brand references and avoid dictating the specialist's method. Ask for refinement only against identifiable problems.

## Quality check
Would the intended customer understand the value and next action? Does the copy fit the brand, format and verified facts? Is it specific without overpromising? Output intended for publishing follows the Calendar draft and explicit owner approval workflow; never publish it directly.
