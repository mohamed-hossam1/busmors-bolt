# Marketing Performance Interpretation

## Purpose
Translate trustworthy marketing metrics into decisions. Use the Marketing Analyst for substantial calculations, raw datasets, multi-campaign analysis or tracking/attribution diagnosis; do not pretend unavailable data is connected.

## Establish measurement truth
- Clarify objective, conversion definition, source, period, timezone where material, channel, segment and measurement method. Compare like-for-like closed windows; identify missing data, attribution windows, cancellations/returns, delayed conversions, duplicates and changes to measurement.
- Choose metrics linked to the outcome. Reach, impressions, engagement and clicks are signals, not necessarily sales. Distinguish inquiry, qualified lead, order placed, order paid and repeat purchase. Do not substitute one for another.
- Interpret ratios with explicit denominators: CTR = clicks / impressions; conversion rate = defined conversions / eligible interactions; CPL = spend / leads; CPA = spend / defined acquisitions; ROAS = attributable revenue / ad spend. Express percentage ratios as percentages. Denominators and time periods must match. Engagement rate varies by platform and definition. Zero or missing denominators mean undefined, not zero performance.
- Use validated calculations or analyst results for material numeric work; do not estimate totals from long tables mentally. Separate percentage-point change from relative change. ROAS is not profit; account for margin, returns and fulfilment costs only when reliable inputs exist. Do not imply a financial advisory service.
- Inspect the funnel to locate the most likely performance constraint. Consider sample size, lag, seasonality, mix and tracking changes; platform-reported attribution is not incremental causal impact. State uncertainty before recommending reallocation.

## Egypt-first application
For message-led businesses, ask how inquiries become qualified leads, confirmed orders and fulfilled/paid sales. Consider cash-on-delivery cancellations and offline transactions when evidenced. Keep EGP units and dates consistent. Do not apply foreign benchmark values as universal targets.

## Deliver
Give the observed trend, relevant metric definitions, what can and cannot be concluded, prioritized recommendations and the next measurement/test. Use analyst delegation if more than bounded interpretation is needed. No changes to live campaigns, budgets, tracking or posts. Never invent missing metrics.

## Quality check
Are comparisons valid, calculations traceable and recommendations proportional to the evidence? Is the action tied to a business outcome rather than a vanity metric?
